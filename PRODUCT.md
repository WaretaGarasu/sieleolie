# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local businesses (hotels, restaurants, hospitality venues), homeowners, public entities, and industrial contractors across the Aeolian Islands — primarily Italian-speaking, with a secondary English-speaking audience (tourists, expats, foreign clients). Users are making high-stakes decisions about electrical systems and fire safety: they need to trust the provider before they pick up the phone.

## Product Purpose

Brochure/portfolio site that converts visitors into contact inquiries. Primary goals: establish technical credibility (ICIM certification, 27+ years, Stromboli emergency story), communicate service breadth, and make it frictionless to reach Siel Eolie by phone, WhatsApp, or email. Success = a phone call or message.

## Brand Personality

Competente, autentica, affidabile. Voice is direct and warm — expert tradespeople talking to real people, not a marketing department talking at prospects. Content is grounded in facts (dates, certifications, specific islands served), never vague superlatives.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS / startup aesthetic: rounded cards, cream backgrounds, "everything is Inter," feature-grid layouts borrowed from software marketing.
- Tourist-trap Mediterranean: over-designed sun/sea/white clichés that feel like a travel brochure, not an electrical contractor.
- Overcrowded SME sites: local-builder-platform clutter — mismatched fonts, too many competing colours, badge overload.

## Design Principles

1. **Local credibility over generic polish** — The Aeolian context and ICIM certification are the site's strongest differentiators. Design surfaces these facts; decoration is secondary.
2. **Trust through clarity** — Visitors are evaluating a contractor for important work. Simplicity and directness signal competence; visual complexity creates doubt.
3. **Mediterranean restraint, not cliché** — The sea-blue palette honors the setting without resorting to travel-brochure aesthetics. Color is purposeful and restrained.
4. **Technical honesty** — Real work (portfolio photos), real certifications, real contact details. No stock imagery, no abstract filler.
5. **Accessible by necessity** — Older clients, island connectivity constraints, mixed device profiles. Performance and accessibility are non-negotiable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Site already implements: skip links, ARIA labels throughout, reduced-motion media query, noscript fallback banner, focus-visible styles, semantic HTML. Maintain and extend these.
